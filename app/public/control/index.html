How does scoring work?

Click Won on a team when they take a game, map or set. For Smash crew battles, turn on the stock counter: each team shares a pool of stocks, players go in the order listed under Rosters, and the first player with stocks left is shown as on stage.

  1. Click Lost a stock each time a player loses a stock. Undo reverses a misclick.
  2. When a team runs out, click Won on the winning team. It adds a point and refills both teams' stocks.
  3. Keyboard: 1 and 2 take a stock from each team, Shift+1 and Shift+2 undo, 3 and 4 give each team a win.

Rocket League reads the game itself: goals, clock, boost and stats come from Rocket League's Stats API, and each finished game adds a win to its team automatically. Blue is the left side: if team A is playing orange, click Swap sides. Use the + and − buttons to correct a series score.

How do I set this up?

OBS can host this control panel as a dock, right beside your scenes. It is the same panel, just embedded in the OBS window.

  1. In OBS, open Docks then Custom Browser Docks…
  2. Give it a name, such as Widener Control.
  3. Paste the URL below, then click Apply.

OBS scene-sync Optional

What does scene-sync do?

It connects to the WebSocket server built into OBS 28 and newer, then builds one scene per overlay. After that, every Push Live switches OBS to the matching scene using OBS's own transition, such as the Widener stinger. Switching scenes by hand in OBS moves the panel's red LIVE dot to match.

Once connected, the app reconnects on its own if OBS is closed or restarted, and connects automatically when the app starts. Leave this disconnected to keep the single-source setup.

  1. In OBS, open Tools then WebSocket Server Settings.
  2. Enable the server and copy the password.
  3. Fill in the fields below and click Connect.
  4. Click Build / update scenes, then turn scene-sync on.

Background music i

How does the music play?

The included track downloads from the team Google Drive (36 MB) the first time it's needed, like the game montages. Or point it at any audio file on this PC.

OBS plays it, through one media source called WU: Music that Build / update scenes (under scene-sync above) adds to every WU scene. Being the same source in every scene, it carries on through scene changes and transitions instead of starting over. The app fades it up and down, so OBS must be connected here.
